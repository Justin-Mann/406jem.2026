Brand Foundation

Rooted in purpose.

Salutaris — from the Latin salutaris, meaning healthful, beneficial, curative. Every product is a considered formulation. Every consultation, a craft.

01

Purity

Pre-approved organic carrier and essential oils only. No fillers, no shortcuts. Every ingredient earns its place in the formula.

02

Precision

Custom blends formulated for targeted application — skin, hair, or aromatic. The right oil for the right purpose, calibrated to the individual.

03

Craft

Small-batch, hands-on production. Whether a bespoke personal blend or a limited product run, the standard never changes.

"Salutaris exists for those who understand that what goes on the body matters as much as what goes in it. We don't cut corners — we blend with intent."

Mark

A geometric botanical tree: triangle canopy with radiating branch lines (2 per side), two-leg root base. Reads as both plant and mountain — organic and grounded. Linework only; no fills in the primary mark.

  • Forest green (#243D2E) on light backgrounds
  • Cream (#F0E8D6) on Forest or Charcoal
  • Branches in Gold (#9B7B3D) for two-tone treatment
  • Scales cleanly from 10mm label to signage

Wordmark

SALUTARIS in wide-tracked EB Garamond (400), with "Custom Organic Oils" in Jost Light below, separated by a hairline gold rule. Used in stacked (labels/packaging) and horizontal (letterhead/cards) lockups.

  • EB Garamond 400, letter-spacing 8–10px
  • Jost 200–300 for subtitle, letter-spacing 4px
  • Tagline: Pure by nature. Precise by design.
  • Gold rule (#9B7B3D) separates name from subtitle

Product Label System

The label is the
first impression.

Labels are Salutaris's primary customer-facing deliverable. Each label must communicate purity, precision, and professionalism — at any size, from a 10ml roll-on to a 100ml bottle.

Label Information Hierarchy

1
Brand Name

SALUTARIS — always present, small caps, Jost 500, tracked wide

2
Product / Blend Name

Largest element — EB Garamond 400, natural name (e.g. "Rosehip Facial Oil")

3
Product Type / Collection

Jost 300, tracked uppercase — e.g. "SKIN OIL" or "AROMATIC BLEND"

4
Key Ingredients

Jost 200–300, small — e.g. "Rosehip · Jojoba · Vitamin C & E"

5
Volume & Batch Code

Bottom of label — smallest text. Volume left, batch code right

6
Application Notes (if space allows)

Brief usage note or "For external use only" — Jost 200, micro size

Brand Voice

How Salutaris speaks.

Authoritative without being clinical. Warm without being casual. The voice carries the weight of expertise lightly — never overwhelming, always considered.

Precise & Knowledgeable

We know our craft. Copy should reflect deep formulation knowledge without lecturing. Let the ingredients and process speak with quiet confidence.

"Formulated with cold-pressed rosehip seed oil — selected for its concentration of trans-retinoic acid and omega fatty acids, not its label appeal."
Avoid: "This oil is AMAZING and great for your skin! Everyone loves it!"

Warm & Personal

Every blend starts with a conversation. The copy should feel like it comes from a formulator who cares — not a marketing team. First person where appropriate.

"Tell us what you're working with. We'll build something that actually fits."
Avoid: "Our proprietary system delivers optimal results for all skin types."

Restrained & Considered

We don't oversell. Every word earns its place — on a label, on a card, in a conversation. Less copy, more weight.

"Rosehip Facial Oil. 30ml. Organic cold-pressed · skin barrier support."
Avoid: "Revolutionary breakthrough formula transforms your skin overnight with visible results!"

Grounded in Nature

Reference the source, not the science jargon. Botanical names welcome. Chemistry in plain terms. The land, the plant, the oil — in that order.

"Cedarwood — harvested slow-grown, steam distilled. Grounding. Long-lasting. The kind of quiet that settles."
Avoid: "Contains advanced phytochemical bioactive compounds for synergistic wellness optimization."
